SafeZone SA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Important safety notice

SafeZone SA is a community safety tool. It does not replace the emergency services. In an emergency, call SAPS 10111, ambulance or fire 10177, or 112 from a cellphone.

1. Who we are

SafeZone SA ("the app", "we", "us") is an Android community safety app for South Africa, developed by the SafeZone SA team. This policy explains what personal information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We process personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Contact: YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL@example.com

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • Full name, email address and mobile number, which you enter when you register.
  • Your password. It is handled by Firebase Authentication, which stores only a salted, hashed version. We never see or store your actual password.
  • If you choose "Continue with Google": your Google account name and email address, supplied by Google. We never receive your Google password.

Location

  • Your precise location, only when you use a feature that needs it: sending an SOS, reporting an incident, sharing your live location or using Walk With Me, the crime map, the area safety score, safe routes, and nearby alerts.

Content you create

  • Incident reports, titles, descriptions, categories, photos you choose to attach, comments, confirmations, community chat messages, and missing-person posts.

Emergency and trusted contacts

  • Names and phone numbers of the emergency or trusted contacts you add or choose, used only so the app can alert them or share your location with them when you ask it to.

Device and technical information

  • A push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send safety alerts to your phone.
  • Basic technical logs, such as error messages, to keep the app working.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To deliver safety features: SOS alerts, incident reports and community verification, the crime map, area safety score, safe routes, Walk With Me, and live location sharing.
  • To send safety notifications you have allowed.
  • To show weather and routing information for your area.
  • To prevent abuse, moderate content and keep the community safe.
  • To answer your support requests and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and the app contains no advertising.

5. Permissions the app asks for

  • Location: to show your area, send your position with an SOS and for safety features. You can deny it, but those features will not work.
  • Notifications: to deliver safety alerts. You can turn this off in the app's Settings or in Android settings.
  • Camera or photos: only when you choose to attach a photo to a report.
  • Contacts: only when you choose someone from your phone to be a trusted contact. We do not read your whole address book.
  • Phone and SMS: to place calls to emergency numbers and to send emergency messages to your contacts when you use SOS.

You can change any permission at any time in your phone's Settings, under Apps, then SafeZone SA, then Permissions.

6. Storage, offline use and retention

  • Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers, and some data (such as cached incidents and reports waiting to sync) is stored on your phone so the app can work offline.
  • We keep your information while your account is active, and for as long afterwards as needed for safety, abuse prevention or legal reasons. You can ask us to delete it at any time (see section 8).

7. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards: encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the app and our servers, hashed passwords, and access rules on our database. No system is completely secure, so please keep your phone and account protected.

8. Your rights, and how to delete your account and data

Under POPIA you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, object to its use, or have it deleted.

  • In the app: open Profile, then Settings, to update your details or delete your account.
  • By email: write to YOUR-SUPPORT-maphotokgethi@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my SafeZone SA account". We will confirm and delete your account and associated personal data, normally within 30 days. Some anonymised safety information may remain.

If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, you may also complain to South Africa's Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

9. Children

SafeZone SA is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we will also notify you in the app.
